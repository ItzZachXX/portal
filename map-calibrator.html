Map Calibrator

Turns clicks on the map picture plus in-game coordinates into the mapping your radio or CAD system needs. Everything runs in your browser. Nothing is uploaded.

1. Add a point

Stand somewhere recognisable in the game, note your coordinates, then click that exact spot on the map. Use spots far apart, opposite corners is best. Three or more points lets this check its own accuracy.

Enter X and Z, then click the map.
